# Accessibility Review Checklist

## Purpose

Review human-facing UI against the default WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility baseline.

Canonical principle source: `.wwg/wiki/principles/accessibility-principles.md`.
Guidance source: `.wwg/wiki/design-system/accessibility-baseline.md`.

## Checklist

- [ ] Can the surface be used with keyboard only?
- [ ] Are focus indicators visible and predictable?
- [ ] Do text, icons, controls, and status indicators meet contrast expectations?
- [ ] Do images, icons, and non-text content have useful alternatives?
- [ ] Are headings and landmarks semantic and navigable?
- [ ] Are form labels, instructions, constraints, and required fields clear?
- [ ] Are errors identified near the source and paired with recovery guidance?
- [ ] Does the layout remain usable across supported viewport sizes?
- [ ] Are status updates available in text, not only animation or color?
- [ ] Does motion respect reduced-motion preferences?
- [ ] Are touch targets large enough and spaced safely?
- [ ] Are documented exceptions recorded as WWG accepted decisions?

## Closeout

Accessibility gaps are release risks until fixed or formally accepted.
