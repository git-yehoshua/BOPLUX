# Accessibility Baseline

## Purpose

Provide checklist-level accessibility guidance for implementation and review.

Canonical principle source: `.wwg/wiki/principles/accessibility-principles.md`.

## Default Target

Target WCAG 2.2 AA unless an accepted decision documents a different target.

## Guidance

- Keyboard: every interactive control is reachable and usable without a mouse.
- Focus: focus indicators are visible, predictable, and not hidden by layout.
- Contrast: text, icons, borders, and status indicators meet contrast expectations.
- Labels: inputs, controls, icons, and ambiguous actions have accessible names.
- Headings: headings describe structure and do not skip meaningful levels.
- Landmarks: pages expose meaningful regions for navigation.
- Responsive UI: content reflows without overlap, clipping, or horizontal traps.
- Status announcements: long-running work and important changes are exposed in text.
- Forms: instructions, constraints, validation, and required fields are clear.
- Error messages: errors identify the problem and the next recovery action.
- Reduced motion: nonessential animation respects reduced-motion settings.
- Touch targets: primary controls are large enough and not crowded.

## Exceptions

Accessibility exceptions must be recorded as WWG accepted decisions. Temporary gaps should be tracked as release risks until resolved or formally accepted.
